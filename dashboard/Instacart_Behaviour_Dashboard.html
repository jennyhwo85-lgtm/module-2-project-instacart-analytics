What this means for the business

Draft — to finalise once real query results are in

Workforce planning

Sunday and Monday mornings consistently peak — picking/packing staffing should scale up ahead of these windows rather than spreading evenly across the week.

Repeat-order nudges

Frequent Repeat Customers already reorder over half their basket — a "reorder your usuals" prompt at checkout targets this group with minimal extra effort.

Product availability

A small set of produce and dairy staples carry very high reorder rates — stock-outs on these items likely hurt retention disproportionately more than on long-tail products.

Growing Occasional customers

Occasional segments order infrequently with lower variety — re-engagement campaigns (not discounts on already-loyal items) may lift order frequency more effectively.
